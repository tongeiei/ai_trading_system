config tested — 3y smoke, full 20y baseline, a 13-cell parameter grid

passing; this is not a borderline or single-unlucky-cell result.

Critically, the §6(a) red-flag check required by the plan produced the
opposite of the mechanism's prediction: a NAIVE baseline that fades the
capitulation candle immediately (no exhaustion wait) is strong (PF 2.2-2.4,
94-98% folds positive) while the pre-registered confirm-wait rule is not
just weaker but actively lost the trade. Waiting for "exhaustion" makes
things dramatically worse, not better — the opposite of what the mechanism
story predicts. This is disqualifying on its own terms per the plan's own
kill criterion (§6): the confirm logic must add edge over the naive
baseline; instead it destroys an apparently-strong effect entirely.

R8 (post-liquidation reversal, confirm-wait rule as specified in
R8_PLAN.md §2) = FAIL. Falsified on gate items 1, 3, 4, 5 (only item 6,
sample size, passes — irrelevant once 1/3/4/5 fail). This mirrors R1's

Mechanism note for the backlog: the strongest single finding in this run
is NOT the falsification of the confirm-wait rule per se, but that an
un-registered, un-validated "fade the capitulation candle immediately"
baseline shows a large, consistent, multi-decade edge (PF 2.2-2.4, 94-98%
folds positive, both on the DEV split and full 20y) while the version that
"waits for exhaustion" — the whole mechanism story in §1 — kills it. Two
readings are possible and neither has been tested here: (a) genuine
knife-catching artifact (the R8_PLAN's own explicitly-named risk in §6:
"just knife-catching with extra steps" — except here it's knife-catching
WITHOUT extra steps that looks good, which is the more alarming version of
that risk, not the less alarming one), possibly inflated by SL/TP placed
very close to entry (tight sl_distance from close_frac<=0.35 construction)
interacting with same-bar-ambiguity or quantization in the M1 labeling; or
(b) a real, different edge (pure fade-the-big-red-candle, no confirm) that
was never pre-registered or holdout-tested and must NOT be treated as
validated — it has not been through sensitivity, holdout, or cost-stress
review, and the a-priori mechanism story for it ("who is forced to pay")
is weaker/different from what R8_PLAN.md registered (immediate fade is
closer to "buying the exact bottom of a crash," which the plan explicitly
warned against fading without a confirm filter). This finding should NOT
be reported as an edge; it is a new, unregistered candidate hypothesis
that would need its OWN falsification plan (rule spec, param grid,
sacred DEV/HOLDOUT split not yet touched, cost stress, look-ahead review)
before being taken seriously — exactly the discipline R2 went through and
failed. Flagging for the backlog, not claiming it.
